STEM COURSE INTRODUCTION

1. STEM THEME

Exploring the Eight Planets of the Solar System: Starship Reboot Mission

2. STEM BRANCHES

Science (Astronomy & Planetary Science) & Technology (Interactive Simulation & Systems Troubleshooting)

3. GAME OVERVIEW

Designed for middle school students, this narrative retro pixel-art web game puts players in the role of a starship pilot whose cockpit loses power after a coffee spill during space turbulence. To reach the Storage Room and retrieve the spare control chip, players must unlock 5 planetary security doors by identifying planets, matching Earth's surface gravity (9.8 m/s²), recognizing Martian iron-oxide soil, comparing planetary volumes, and recalling key planetary traits.

4. LEARNING OUTCOMES (LOs)
  • LO1 (Planet Identification & Order): Students will be able to accurately identify all eight planets of the Solar System by visual appearance, relative volume, and sequential position from the Sun.
  • LO2 (Core Planetary Characteristics): Students will be able to recall and match key physical features of each planet (such as Earth's 9.8 m/s² gravity, Mars's red iron-oxide soil, Venus's greenhouse heat and retrograde rotation, Jupiter's massive size and Great Red Spot, Saturn's rings and low density, Uranus's 98° axial tilt, and Neptune's extreme winds).
